# Fork provenance: is the makerrepo fork the "official" build123d openGrid?

Kata: `28fx` (parent `enag`). Audit only. No changes to `src/`, `tests/`, or `output/`.

## Scope

- **Provenance.** Who wrote `../reference_repos/opengrid-build123-makerrepo-fork/`, under what license, and whether the openGrid authors endorse or link it.
- **Dimensions.** Every constant in the fork's `opengrid/constants.py` and every hardcoded dimension in `opengrid/base.py` and `opengrid/multiconnect.py`. Each is compared against:
  - the two reference PDFs;
  - the SCAD sources (QuackWorks, opengrid-projects);
  - David D's official STEP files. These turned out to be the files the fork was reverse-engineered from (see below).
- **Coverage.** Which openGrid items the fork implements and which it does not. Fork-only items are listed, not audited.

## Method

1. **Provenance.**
   - Read the fork's `README.md`, `LICENSE`, `pyproject.toml`, and `.makerrepo/config.yaml`, plus `git log` (54 commits) and `git remote -v`.
   - Checked the commit diffs (`git log -p`) for references to source files.
   - Queried the GitHub API for the history of `src/content/docs/links.mdx` in `openGrid-3D/openGrid-docs` and for PR #27 (author, merger).
2. **Fork geometry.** Built in this repo's uv env with throwaway scripts outside the repo. `mr` (makerrepo) and `pydantic` were stubbed with no-op decorators and models; these stubs affect no geometry.
   - `Base()`, `Base(2,2)`, `Base(2,3)`, `ConnectorCutter()`, `RoundHead()`, `Slot()`, `RoundHeadCutter()`, `SlotCutter()`, `SlotOpeningCutter()`, `SnapInSlot()`, `SnapInSlotCutter()`, `SnapInQuickConnectTopBackCutter()`.
   - Probes: `bounding_box()`; planar XY sections (intersection with a large `Rectangle` face at height z).
     - Cell walls: inner-wire bbox of the section, `wall = (28 − opening)/2`.
     - Corner block: diagonal depth `(28 − max(x+y))/√2`.
     - Outer chamfer: outer-wire vertices on the board edge.
     - Pocket: chord length of a line through the section face at each depth.
     - Radii: half-width of the section bbox at fixed z.
3. **Official STEP.** Each file loaded with `build123d.import_step`; listed circle-edge radii and centres and vertex z-levels; sectioned the same way.
   - Fork vs official: bbox-aligned, then `(fork − off).volume` and `(off − fork).volume`.
   - For parts whose orientation is unknown, searched rotations (X flip × 4 Z rotations × 3 Y anchors) and kept the minimum.
4. **Ours.** Built `open_grid(2, 2, chamfers=CORNERS, connector_holes=True)`, `open_grid(1,1,...)`, `build_connector_slot_delete_tool()`, `build_adjacent_grid_connector()`, `build_multiconnect_profile(...)` and `build_multiconnect_head(...)`, and probed them the same way.

Tolerance: 0.01 mm.

### Verdict on the "official" claim

**Not official. It is a third-party implementation that the project lists, and it is faithful to the official STEPs for what it covers.**

- The fork does not claim to be official.
- It is authored, copyrighted, and hosted by a third party (Fang-Pen Lin / Launch Platform).
- The openGrid docs maintainer accepted a self-submitted link to it into the "useful links" page. That is a listing, not authorship or a normative status. openGrid's normative artifacts remain David D's Printables/MakerWorld files and PDFs.

Geometrically, the fork is the closest of all sources to the official STEP files:

- The board, connector negative, round/slot negatives, and slot opening match the official STEPs with symmetric-difference volume 0.
- It deviates in three places: the missing Multiconnect dimple, Snap-In head spacing 0.795 vs 0.759, and Snap-In negative slot length 28 vs 26.5.

No fork value contradicts either PDF. Where the fork disagrees with the SCAD ports (connector pocket, slot-opening radius, snap-in triangle inset), the official STEP agrees with the fork, not with the SCAD.

## Coverage

**Implemented by the fork:**

- Full board (`Base`), with per-edge connector pockets and outer corner chamfers (always all four).
- Connector negative (`ConnectorCutter`).
- Multiconnect `RoundHead`, `Slot`, `RoundHeadCutter`, `SlotCutter`, `SlotOpeningCutter`, `SnapInSlot`, `SnapInSlotCutter`, `SnapInQuickConnectTopBackCutter`.

**Not implemented (coverage gaps vs PDFs / SCAD / this repo):**

- openGrid Lite (4 mm) and Heavy (13.8 mm) boards; adhesive base; stacking / interface layer; fill-space.
- Screw holes (R2.05 / countersink, PDF-T detail D) and every `Screw_Mounting` mode; per-corner chamfer toggles and "Everywhere" chamfers.
- Positive adjacent/edge connector; connector insert tool.
- Snaps (bare / directional / lock / lite), snap threads, self-expanding snaps.
- openConnect head/screw/slots; Multiconnect threaded insert (PDF-M); Multiconnect rails/backers/receivers with dimples and on-ramp spacing (QW `multiconnectGenerator.scad`); Multiconnect Jr / Mini profiles.

**Fork-only or extra items (listed, not audited):**

- `ZipTieModule` (`zip_tie.py:10-36`; no official counterpart).
- Version-mark text (constants.py:8-9).
- `snap_in_slot_cutter_fitting_test` (multiconnect.py:463-498, 6.4 mm plate).
- `SnapInSlot`, `SlotOpeningCutter`, `SnapInQuickConnectTopBackCutter`: fork-only relative to PDFs and SCAD, but they are official modeling files (spot-checked above).
- `NEGATIVE_MODEL_COLOR` (cosmetic).

## Fork values contradicting the PDFs

**None.** Every fork value that PDF-T dimensions matches it within 0.01 mm: 28, 6.8, 1.1, 0.4, 0.8/0.7, 1/1, 2.4, 4.2, 2.6, 1.4. PDF-M does not dimension any fork feature.

The fork's disagreements are with:

- **the official STEP:** dimple, 0.795, slot length 26.5;
- **the SCAD ports:** neck arc R2.7951 vs 2.7, R0.5 fillet, mouth 5.134 vs 5.3, on-ramp R11 vs 11.65/11.9/12, triangle inset 0.6 vs 0.4. In each of these the official STEP sides with the fork.

## Discrepancies

1. **Ours wrong (vs official STEP): connector pocket.**
   - `_connector_slot_delete_tool_base` (`opengrid.py:863-885`) follows QW's R2.7 dimple and has neither the R0.5 fillet nor the R0.25 flare.
   - Mouth 4.800 vs 5.134; neck width at depth 1.0 is 5.184 vs 4.980.
   - Tool volume 56.027 vs 55.562 mm³.
   - Also reflected in boards: ours 2x2 vs official differs by +0.563 / −1.964 mm³.
   - The proposed SCAD-flare fix `0wy8` (mouth 5.3) would not reach the official geometry either. `0wy8` has since been closed as superseded by `jmq8`.
2. **Ours wrong (vs official STEP): positive connector length.** 9.8 vs 9.9227, V 94.94 vs 95.87.
3. **Ours, already filed (`smfm`): head dimple on the wrong face.** Official STEP and OGP `:378-379` put a 1 mm cone recess in the R10 face. Ours head bbox is 3.99 tall, which is at the tolerance limit.
4. **Spec ambiguous: Multiconnect on-ramp top radius.** Ours 11.65 (QW generator `:192`); official modeling STEP 11.0; QW BOSL 11.9; old QW design 12.0 with h=5.
5. **Fork wrong: no detent dimple** (R1.05→R0.05 × 1) on `RoundHead` / `Slot` / `SnapInSlot`.
6. **Fork wrong: `SnapInSlotCutter` second-head / triangle offset** 0.795 vs official 0.759.
7. **Fork wrong: `SnapInSlotCutter` slot length** 28 vs official 26.5 behind the head centre.
8. **SCAD disagrees with official STEP** (informational, for tie-breaks): QW connector neck R2.7 and flare 5.3; QW snap-in triangle inset 0.4; QW on-ramp radii.
9. **Provenance:** the fork is not authored or hosted by openGrid. It is only linked from opengrid.world via a self-submitted PR (#27) merged by the docs maintainer. Treat it as a high-fidelity third-party port, not ground truth.

## Proposed fix issues

- **Filed `jmq8`** (related `28fx`, `0wy8`): align `build_connector_slot_delete_tool` / board pockets and `build_adjacent_grid_connector` with the official `openGrid Edge Connector (Negative).step`, or explicitly declare QW SCAD normative. A comment on `0wy8` points to it.
- Not filed, already tracked: head dimple face (`smfm`). The missing-SCAD-flare issue (`0wy8`) was closed by AdjacentConnector as superseded by `jmq8`.
- Not filed (spec ambiguous): on-ramp radius. A project-level decision is needed on whether the official modeling STEPs or the QW generator are normative for Multiconnect receivers.
- Not filed (fork bugs, outside this repo): fork dimple omission, 0.795 → 0.759, snap-in negative slot length 26.5. These would go upstream to `makerrepo.com/r/fangpenlin/opengrid` if desired.

## Resolutions

- `#jmq8`: the pocket and the positive connector now match the official Edge Connector STEPs (symmetric difference 0 mm³ for both, and against the official 2×2 pocket region). The same pocket is used for Full, Lite and Heavy.
